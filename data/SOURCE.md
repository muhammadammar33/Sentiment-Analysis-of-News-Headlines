# Dataset source

**News Headlines Dataset for Sarcasm Detection** (version 2)

- File: `Sarcasm_Headlines_Dataset_v2.json` (JSON-lines, 28,619 records)
- Downloaded from: https://github.com/rishabhmisra/News-Headlines-Dataset-For-Sarcasm-Detection
- Also on Kaggle: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/rmisra/news-headlines-dataset-for-sarcasm-detection
- Origin: real news headlines from *HuffPost* and satirical headlines from *The Onion*

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `is_sarcastic` | 1 = The Onion (satire), 0 = HuffPost (real news) |
| `headline` | headline text (lower case) |
| `article_link` | URL of the original article |
